Recently, I heard about CRDT[fn:1] a lot from some podcasts:

- [[https://changelog.com/podcast/147][The Changelog #147: Elixir and Phoenix with Chris McCord | Changelog | News and podcasts for developers]]
- [[http://5by5.tv/rubyonrails/239][5by5 | Ruby on Rails Podcast #239: When You Type I Type We Type: Part 2]]

Turns out, CRDT is used in Phoenix for its PubSub[fn:2] module and in Teletype for Atom for its text syncing process[fn:3].

If you are interested in this kind of reliable distributed system, definitely go check it out: [[https://twitter.com/chris_mccord/status/711934423877480448][Chris McCord on Twitter: "If you're
curious about the CRDT in my Erlang Factory keynote for Phoenix
Presence, our impl is based on this paper https://t.co/q6CDmPB4Mj"]].
