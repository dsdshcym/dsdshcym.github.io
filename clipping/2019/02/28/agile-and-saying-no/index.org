#+TITLE: Agile and Saying "NO"
#+begin_quote

- The 4Ds Time Management

  1. Delete/Drop
  2. Delegate
  3. Defer
  4. Do

- The First D: Delete or Drop

  1. Maintain a Not-to-do List
  2. Stop Doing
  3. You Don't Have To
  4. Stop Completing Surveys
  5. Drop That Call
  6. Stop Starting

     - Start finishing projects that are in-progress
     - Work on as few major tasks as you can on any given day (This principle is adopted from agile project management.)

#+end_quote

I think the mindset to say NO to everything first matches the idea of Agile Software Development pretty well.

To me, part of being Agile is to validate idea before working on it and to say "no" to it as soon as we find it will not work[fn:1].

Being Agile sometimes means pursuing some sorts of minimalism. And achieving the expected results by doing nothing is the best, isn't it?[fn:2]
