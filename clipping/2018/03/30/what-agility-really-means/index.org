#+TITLE: What Agility Really Means
#+begin_quote

- What to Do

  1. Find out where you are
  2. Take a small step towards your goal
  3. Adjust your understanding based on what you learned
  4. Repeat

- How to Do It

  - When faced with two of more alternatives that deliver roughly the same value, take the path that makes future change easier

#+end_quote

-- from [[https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=a-BOSpxYJ9M][GOTO 2015 • Agile is Dead • Pragmatic Dave Thomas - YouTube]] and [[https://pragdave.me/blog/2014/03/04/time-to-kill-agile.html][Agile is Dead (Long Live Agility)]]

I think this can be the summary of my current understanding of Agility.

*Only by taking steps as small as possible, can we embrace the changes this interesting world presents us without losing any sanity.* [fn:1]

And this agility thinking can absolutely be adopted to all the things we are doing at work or even in our daily life:

1. TDD
2. Issue Solving
3. Feature Management
4. Company Management
5. Skill Learning

Also, this methodology reflects TDD perfectly:

Find out where you are
    Write a failing test
Take a small step towards your goal
    Fix this failing test
Adjust your understanding based on what you learned
    Refactor or Start considering next test
Repeat

I first learned this idea from [[https://www.sandimetz.com/99bottles/][99 Bottles - Sandi Metz]]. (You can also read my review of this book [[http://dsdshcym.github.io/blog/2017/12/08/book-review-99-bottles-of-oop/#orgb711f32][here]][fn:2])
