#+TITLE: Your editor configuration is the biggest project in your life

I've been thinking about whether to use a distribution (like [[https://github.com/syl20bnr/spacemacs][Spacemacs]][fn:1], [[https://github.com/hlissner/doom-emacs][doom-emacs]][fn:2]) or to use my own configurations ([[https://github.com/dsdshcym/.emacs.d][dsdshcym/.emacs.d]]).

Watch this talk if you are facing the similar problem as I'm.

#+begin_quote

- What are the attributes of a good editor?

  1. Community
  2. Extensibility
  3. Portability

- How to spot a bad editor?

  1. Close sourced
  2. Bad plugins
  3. Bad docs

- *Your editor configuration is the biggest project in your life*

#+end_quote

-- from [[https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=aWa0S60f530][LinuxDays 2017 - Why good editors refuse to die - David Bečvařík - YouTube]]
